Bath Bath is just a World Heritage Site, one of the very famous tourist destinations outside London. It's a spa town in Somerset a handful of hours from London. It has been a settlement since Roman times (Bath being named for the Roman Baths there), and is famed for its hot springs, the only naturally occuring hot springs in Britain. It became popular as a vacation resort in the Georgian era, that will be when a lot of Bath was assembled, including architectural gems such as the Royal Crescent. Visitors can visit the Roman Baths and simply take the waters (i.e. drink it) in addition to tour the various portions of the undergrounds bathrooms and visit the museum.
